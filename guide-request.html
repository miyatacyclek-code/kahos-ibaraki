Want a local to show you Ibaraki?

by Kaho · I live in Ibaraki, 1 hour from Tokyo

Hi! I'm Kaho. I share real life in rural Japan on YouTube and TikTok. Many of you ask me, “Can you show me around?” So here is a page for that.

Tell me when you want to come and what you like. I'll read every request and reply by email. Sending a request is free and is not a booking yet.

Ideas for your day

  • Blue hills & ocean torii
    Hitachi Seaside Park, seafood at Nakaminato, sunset at Oarai.
  • Waterfall & autumn leaves
    Fukuroda Falls, Ryujin Suspension Bridge, countryside food.
  • Real rural life
    Local supermarket, farm stands, natto and hoshiimo. Ask me anything about life here!

How it works

  1. Send a request with this form. I'll reply by email with ideas and the price for your plan.
  2. Short video call. We say hi and decide the plan together.
  3. Pay in advance to confirm your day. I'll send you a payment link.
  4. Meet me at a train station in Ibaraki, in the daytime. Let's go!

Good to know

  • We travel by train, bus or taxi. I don't drive guests in my car.
  • Train and bus fares, tickets and meals are not included. Please also cover my fares, tickets and meals on the day.
  • Small groups only (up to 4 people), so everyone can talk and ask questions.

Unwritten rules of the Japanese countryside

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Living in rural Japan comes with rules that are not written anywhere. Here are a few.

  • Say hello to everyone. Even people you don't know. Especially old people.
  • When you travel, bring back omiyage (souvenirs) for coworkers and neighbors.
  • Join the neighborhood association (jichikai). Cleaning days are not really optional 😅
  • If someone gives you something, return the favor — but not right away, and not too much.
  • Don't park in front of someone's field. They will find you.

It sounds strict, but it's how people take care of each other.

Have you noticed any unwritten rules in Japan? Let me know!

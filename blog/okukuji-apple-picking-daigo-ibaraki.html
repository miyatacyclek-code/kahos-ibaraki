Okukuji apples: apple picking in Daigo, Ibaraki

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

When people think of Japanese apples, they think of Aomori or Nagano. But Ibaraki has apples too! In Daigo town, in the mountains of north Ibaraki, there are many small apple orchards. The apples here are called Okukuji apples (奥久慈りんご).

What are Okukuji apples?

Okukuji is the name of the mountain area along the upper Kuji River. Many orchards in Daigo let the apples ripen on the tree before they pick them. The official Ibaraki guide says these apples are “packed with juicy sweetness.” Orchards grow several varieties, from classic ones to new ones.

Pick a red apple from the tree, with autumn mountains all around you.

When is apple picking season in Ibaraki?

Autumn, from about September to November, is the fruit harvest time in Ibaraki. Most Daigo orchards open for picking from late September or October, and many close around the end of November or early December. So mid-November is your last chance this year! Dates can change, so please check each orchard before you go.

Orchards to know

  • Himegataki Apple Orchards: between the Kuji River and Tsukimachi Falls. All-you-can-eat apple picking. Closed on Wednesdays.
  • Fujita Orchard: on a hilltop, and famous for its apple pie.
  • Kuroda Orchard: an orchard with about 75 years of history.
  • Honda Orchard: nice views and autumn leaves.

Many farm websites are only in Japanese. A translation app helps a lot!

Apples and a hot spring

Fukuroda Falls is very close. And from October 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027, Fukuroda Onsen Omoide Romankan has an “Apple Event” with a bath scented with Okukuji apples. Falls, apples, onsen. A perfect autumn day!

How to get there

  • By train: Ueno to Mito is about 1 hour 10 minutes. Then take the JR Suigun Line from Mito to Hitachi-Daigo, about 1 hour 20 minutes.
  • Himegataki Apple Orchards is about 10 minutes by taxi from Hitachi-Daigo Station.
  • By car: about 1 hour from Naka IC on the Joban Expressway.

Trains on the Suigun Line are not very frequent, so check the timetable first.

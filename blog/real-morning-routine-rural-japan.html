My real morning in rural Japan: no fancy life

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

This week I shared two videos of my mornings, and many of you asked about them. So here is my real morning, before work, in rural Japan. No fancy life, I promise!

6am: I wake up (slowly)

My alarm rings at 6am. I am not a morning person at all 😴 In November it is still a little dark and cold, so getting out of the futon is the hardest part of my day.

Skincare and coffee

First, simple skincare. Then coffee. On one morning this week I ran out of coffee… and the day felt very long 😂

Homemade onigiri

I make onigiri (rice balls) at home. It is cheap, quick and easy to eat anywhere. Japanese rice is still good when it is cold, so onigiri is a perfect breakfast or lunch.

Breakfast by the water, before the world wakes up.

Breakfast by the water

Sometimes I take my breakfast outside and eat by the water before work. Rice fields, quiet roads and autumn colors. It is only 15 minutes, but it makes me feel calm for the whole day.

A quiet walk and my neighbor

On other mornings I go for a short walk at 7am. No traffic, no crowds. Just rice fields, mountains and silence. Most of the rice is already harvested now. On the way, I often have a little chat with my neighbor. In the countryside, everyone says good morning.

Then, work

After that, I go to my office job like many people in Japan. Some people say rural life is boring, and sometimes it is. But I love these quiet mornings.

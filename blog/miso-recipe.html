Miso Soup 味噌汁

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

My mom's miso soup = the taste of home. Every family's is a little different.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 400 ml Dashi stock
  • 2 tbsp Miso paste
  • 100 g Silken tofu, cubed
  • 1 tsp Dried wakame seaweed
  • 1 tbsp Green onion, chopped

How to make it

  1. Heat the dashi in a pot until it's almost boiling.
  2. Add the tofu and wakame, cook 1–2 minutes.
  3. Turn the heat low. Put the miso in a ladle and melt it slowly into the soup.
  4. Don't boil it after adding miso — it loses the nice smell.
  5. Serve with green onion on top.

Living outside Japan?

Instant dashi powder (1 tsp + 400 ml water) is totally fine. That's what most Japanese homes use!

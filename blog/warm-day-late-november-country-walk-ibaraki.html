A warm day in late November

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Lately, it has been really cold in Japan.

Yesterday morning in Mito, it was only 1°C (34°F). Earlier this month, the mountain towns of northern Ibaraki went below 0°C for the first time this season. Frosty mornings. Cold hands.

But today was different. The high in Mito was 18°C (64°F), warm for late November. No coat. Just a fleece.

At lunch, I went for a walk on a quiet country road. Blue sky. Dry grass. Old houses with tiled roofs. No cars. No people. Just the wind.

I lived in Tokyo for a few years. Busy, fast, always tired. Here, a simple walk feels like a gift.

Winter mornings in Ibaraki are cold, but on sunny days the afternoon feels almost like spring.

In the countryside, the best thing is not a place to visit. It’s time to breathe.

A weekend from Ibaraki: swans at Lake Inawashiro and a wine harvest festival

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Sometimes the best part of living in Ibaraki is how easy it is to leave for a weekend. Last weekend I drove north.

Morning at Lake Inawashiro

Lake Inawashiro in Fukushima is one of the biggest lakes in Japan. I got there early in the morning. The sky was clear, the water was calm, and Mt. Bandai stood behind the lake.

On the beach there were ducks everywhere, and white swans too. Every winter, swans fly here from the north and stay until spring.

Clear sky, calm water, and swans on the beach.

A harvest festival at Satoyama Wine

The next day I went to the harvest festival at Satoyama Wine in Tochigi. People sat on yellow grape crates between the vines, drinking new wine under a grey November sky.

I really love their wine.

Lunch on the way home

On the way back, I stopped at the outlet mall in Sano for a simple karaage set: fried chicken, rice and miso soup.

Would you rather wake up by the lake, or spend the day at the winery? Tell me on my TikTok, or post your weekend photo with #KahoIbaraki!

Ryujin Suspension Bridge: Ibaraki’s dragon gorge in autumn

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

In north Ibaraki, in the mountains of Hitachiota City, there is a long bridge just for walking. It hangs over a deep valley called Ryujin Gorge (竜神峡). In November, the whole valley under your feet turns red and yellow.

This is Ryujin Suspension Bridge (竜神大吊橋, Ryujin Otsuribashi). If you like autumn leaves and a little bit of a thrill, this place is for you.

What is Ryujin Suspension Bridge?

  • It is 375 meters long, and it is only for people on foot. It is one of the longest pedestrian bridges in Japan.
  • It is about 100 meters above the water of the Ryujin Dam lake.
  • It is in Okukuji Prefectural Natural Park, in a V-shaped valley made by the Ryujin River.

Why “Ryujin”? The dragon story

“Ryujin” means “dragon god.” There is an old legend that two dragons lived in this valley long ago. And when you look from above, the river winds through the gorge like a dragon’s body. I love that the name comes from a story.

Under your feet: 100 meters of air, and a valley on fire with color.

When is the best time for autumn leaves?

The leaves usually turn in November. In 2026 the 37th Ryujin Gorge Autumn Leaves Festival (竜神峡紅葉まつり) runs from November 1 to November 30. There is a local food and products market, and small events on many days, like music and street performances. Leaf status sites said the leaves were at their best in mid-November, so now is a good time. The color changes every year, so check before you go.

How much does it cost?

For the 2026 festival, crossing the bridge is ¥320 for adults (high school age and up) and ¥210 for children. Prices can change, so check the official info before your trip.

Bungee jumping from the bridge

If you are brave, you can bungee jump from the bridge, about 100 meters down. It is said to be one of the highest bungee jumps in Japan. Just watching people jump is exciting too!

How to get there

  • By train and bus: go to JR Hitachiota Station (Suigun Line), then take an Ibaraki Kotsu bus to the “Ryujin Otsuribashi” stop. The ride is about 40 minutes.
  • By car: about 40 minutes from Hitachi-Minami-Ota IC or Naka IC on the Joban Expressway. Parking is free.
  • Buses in the countryside are not frequent. Check the timetable first, and check your bus back too.

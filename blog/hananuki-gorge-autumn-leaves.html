Hananuki Gorge: Ibaraki’s red bridge of autumn leaves

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

If you search for autumn leaves in Ibaraki, one name comes up again and again: Hananuki Gorge (花貫渓谷). It is in Takahagi city, in the north of the prefecture. In autumn, the valley turns red, orange and yellow. This week it is even number one in Walker Plus’s Ibaraki autumn leaves ranking!

What is Hananuki Gorge?

It is a deep V-shaped valley along the Hananuki River. The gorge goes from Hananuki Dam to the Kotakizawa camp site, and there are walking trails along the river. It is also part of the Ibaraki Kenpoku Geopark. Walking here is free, and you can visit any time of year.

The Shiomi Falls suspension bridge

The most famous spot is the Shiomi Falls suspension bridge (汐見滝吊り橋). It is about 60 meters long. You stand in the middle, the bridge moves a little, and maple trees are all around you. Below, you can see the river and the small waterfall.

Stand on a swinging bridge, surrounded by red maple leaves.

When is the best time to see the autumn leaves?

Usually, the leaves are best from mid to late November. This year, tenki.jp says the leaves are at their best now, and it expects good colors until late November or early December. Leaves change every day, so check a leaf report before you go.

Festival and light-up in 2026

  • Hananuki Gorge Momiji Festival: October 31 to November 30, 2026, with food stalls and an information booth.
  • Light-up: November 7 to 23, 2026, from sunset to 7pm.
  • Parking is free most of the year, but you pay during the festival.

How to get there

  • By train: take the JR Joban Line to Takahagi Station. From there it is about 20 minutes by taxi.
  • In the leaf season, shuttle buses run from the station. Check the times before you go.
  • By car: about 15 minutes from Takahagi IC on the Joban Expressway.

Weekends in November get very busy. If you can, go on a weekday morning. Bring warm clothes too, because the valley gets cold.

Hoshiimo season is here!

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

When the wind gets cold, Ibaraki starts making hoshiimo — dried sweet potato. Ibaraki makes most of the hoshiimo in Japan.

Farmers steam sweet potatoes, slice them and dry them in the cold sun. The result is chewy, sticky and naturally sweet. No sugar added!

It's like candy, but healthy. Dangerous.

You can find it at roadside stations (michi-no-eki) and supermarkets in winter. Or try making it at home — the recipe is in the Cook tab!

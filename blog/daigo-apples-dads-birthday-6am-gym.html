Yesterday and this morning: apples, leaves, cake and the gym

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Today I want to look back at yesterday and this morning. Two very different days, but both made me happy.

Yesterday: lunch first

We started with lunch. A big bowl of tanmen (salty ramen with lots of vegetables) and a small steak set with rice and miso soup. Simple Japanese lunch, but so good on a cold day.

Yesterday: apple picking in Daigo

Then we drove to Daigo, a mountain town in the north of Ibaraki. We went to Toyoda Apple Farm, and there were so many boxes of red apples. Red, red, red everywhere!

In Daigo, many farms leave the apples on the tree until they are really ripe, so they are very sweet. One apple was yellow inside and tasted like honey. I said “oishii!” many times.

Yesterday: autumn leaves

After the apples, we walked up a quiet hill with old stone lion statues. From the top, I could see the whole town between the mountains. The maple trees were deep red and orange.

There was a small wooden rest house, so I sat there for a while. I didn't film anything. I just looked. No crowds, no noise.

Sometimes the best moment is the one you don't film.

Yesterday: my dad's birthday

In the evening, we had a birthday party for my dad. The food was so big! A mountain of karaage (Japanese fried chicken) and a birthday cake covered with fruit: strawberries, melon, kiwi, dragon fruit, blueberries… I was so full, I couldn't move. Happy birthday, Dad!

This morning: 6am gym

And after eating that much… this morning I went to the gym at 6am! It was still a little dark and very cold. I wanted to stay in my futon, but I told myself “just go.”

I have a full-time job and I also make videos, so morning is the only time that is really mine. The gym is quiet, and my head feels clear for the whole day.

After my workout, I drank a protein shake with ice. Protein after a workout tastes 100 times better than usual. Best moment of the morning!

My little look back

  • Best food: the honey-sweet yellow apple
  • Best view: red leaves from the quiet hill
  • Best feeling: my dad smiling at his birthday cake
  • Best drink: protein after the 6am gym

Tips if you visit

  • Daigo apple picking: about late September to late November. Check each farm before you go.
  • Autumn leaves in Daigo usually peak in mid to late November.
  • Gyms in Japan: many are open 24 hours, and many ask you to bring clean indoor shoes. Some have tattoo rules, so check first.

Eat a lot, move a lot. I think that is a good balance, haha.

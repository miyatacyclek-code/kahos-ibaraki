Oyakodon 親子丼

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

“Oyako” means parent and child… chicken and egg. A little dark, but so delicious 😂

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 250 g Chicken thigh, bite-size
  • ½ Onion, sliced
  • 3 Eggs
  • 150 ml Dashi stock
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Mirin
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 2 bowls Hot cooked rice

How to make it

  1. Put dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar in a small frying pan and bring to a simmer.
  2. Add the onion and chicken. Cook 5–6 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  3. Beat the eggs lightly — leave some white parts, don't mix too much.
  4. Pour the eggs over in a circle. Cover and cook about 1 minute until half set.
  5. Slide it on top of the rice. Soft and runny is the best!

Living outside Japan?

No mirin? Use 1 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp water instead.

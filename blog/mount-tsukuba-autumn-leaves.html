Mount Tsukuba autumn leaves: Ibaraki’s easy day trip from Tokyo

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Do you want red autumn leaves, but no long trip from Tokyo? Then Mount Tsukuba (筑波山) in Ibaraki is a great choice. It is a mountain with two peaks, and in November the forest turns red and yellow.

When is the best time to see the autumn leaves?

Usually the leaves start to change in late October, and the best time is from early to late November. It changes a little with the weather each year. In mid-November 2026 the color has just started, so the next weeks are a good time to go.

The most popular area is around Tsukubasan Shrine and the path from the shrine to the cable car. You can see ginkgo (yellow) and maple (red) together there.

Is there an evening light-up?

Yes! In 2026 the maple leaves near the cable car are lit up from 17:00 to 20:00. It is on weekends and holidays from October 24 to November 29, and also every day from November 24 to 27. Looking at the light-up is free. You can also ride the cable car at night and see the city lights from the mountain.

Two peaks, red maples, and city lights at night.

How to get to Mount Tsukuba from Tokyo

  • Take the Tsukuba Express from Akihabara to Tsukuba Station (about 45 minutes).
  • From the bus terminal near Tsukuba Station, take the bus to the Tsukubasan-Jinja Iriguchi stop (about 40 minutes).
  • Walk up to Tsukubasan Shrine. The cable car leaves from Miyawaki Station near the shrine.

Cable car, ropeway, or hiking?

The two peaks are Nantaisan (871 m) and Nyotaisan (877 m). The cable car goes up to Nantaisan, and the ropeway from Tsutsujigaoka goes up to Nyotaisan. If you like walking, there are many hiking trails, from easy to hard. It is the lowest of Japan’s “100 famous mountains,” so it is friendly for beginners. Please wear good shoes and bring a warm jacket, because the top is cold in late autumn.

Times and prices change, so check the official Mt. Tsukuba site before you go. On weekends it is busy, so try to go early.

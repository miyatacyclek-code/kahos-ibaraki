Is Ibaraki really Japan's “least attractive” prefecture?

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Every autumn, a big survey ranks Japan's 47 prefectures by how “attractive” they are. And every year, people wait to see if Ibaraki is last again. It was last seven years in a row, from 2013 to 2019, and it has been near the bottom many times since. Even Ibaraki people joke about it.

But honestly? I think the ranking just means people don't know Ibaraki. Not that there's nothing here.

Being unknown is not the same as being boring.

Why does Ibaraki rank so low?

  • It's close to Tokyo, so people visit for a day and don't stay. Fewer memories, less image.
  • There is no single super-famous symbol, like Mount Fuji or Kyoto's temples.
  • Many people mix up Ibaraki with other prefectures near Tokyo. Some people can't even find it on a map!
  • Ibaraki people are shy about selling their own home. We don't advertise much.

What people miss

  • Hills that turn completely blue with nemophila flowers in spring, and red with kochia in autumn (Hitachi Seaside Park).
  • A giant waterfall, one of the three great waterfalls of Japan, that can freeze in winter (Fukuroda Falls).
  • A torii gate standing on the rocks in the ocean (Oarai Isosaki Shrine).
  • Kairakuen in Mito, one of the three great gardens of Japan, famous for plum blossoms in late winter.
  • Mount Tsukuba, an easy mountain with views all the way to Tokyo on a clear day.
  • Ushiku Daibutsu, one of the tallest Buddha statues in the world.
  • Fresh seafood at Nakaminato Fish Market, and anko-nabe (monkfish hot pot) in winter.

Ibaraki food is seriously good

Ibaraki is one of Japan's biggest farming prefectures. It is famous for melons, chestnuts, lotus root, natto from Mito, and hoshiimo (dried sweet potato) — Ibaraki makes most of the hoshiimo in Japan. Many of the vegetables you eat in Tokyo actually come from here.

Easy to reach from Tokyo

Mito is about 1 hour 15 minutes from Tokyo by the limited express train. Tsukuba is about 45 minutes from Akihabara on the Tsukuba Express. And there's Ibaraki Airport too. No crowds like Kyoto. Locals are surprised and happy when foreign people come.

So this is my small project: show the world the real Ibaraki. If you visit, post with #KahoIbaraki. Let's change that ranking together!

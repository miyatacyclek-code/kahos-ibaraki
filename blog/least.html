Is Ibaraki really Japan's “least attractive” prefecture?

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Every autumn, a big survey ranks Japan's 47 prefectures by how “attractive” they are. And every year, people wait to see if Ibaraki is last again. It has been many times. Even Ibaraki people joke about it.

But honestly? I think the ranking just means people don't know Ibaraki. Not that there's nothing here.

Being unknown is not the same as being boring.

What people miss

  • Hills that turn completely blue in spring (Hitachi Seaside Park)
  • A giant waterfall that can freeze in winter (Fukuroda Falls)
  • A torii gate standing in the ocean (Oarai)
  • Some of the best melons, chestnuts and hoshiimo in Japan

And it's about one hour from Tokyo. No crowds like Kyoto. Locals are surprised and happy when foreign people come.

So this is my small project: show the world the real Ibaraki. If you visit, post with #KahoIbaraki. Let's change that ranking together!

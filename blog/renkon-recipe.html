Lotus Root Kinpira れんこんのきんぴら

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Ibaraki grows the most lotus root in Japan! Crunchy, sweet and salty.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 150 g Lotus root
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1½ tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Mirin
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 pinch Chili flakes
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds

How to make it

  1. Peel the lotus root and slice thin (2–3 mm). Soak in water 5 minutes so it stays white.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a pan and stir-fry the lotus root 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add soy sauce, mirin, sugar and chili. Cook until the liquid is almost gone.
  4. Sprinkle sesame seeds. Good hot or cold — great for bento!

Living outside Japan?

Frozen sliced lotus root from Asian supermarkets works well. Carrots or burdock root are also classic.

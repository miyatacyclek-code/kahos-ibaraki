5 things that surprise people about rural Japan

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

When people think of Japan, they think Tokyo: neon, crowded trains, tiny apartments. Rural Japan is a different world. Here's what surprises my followers the most.

1. You need a car

Trains and buses come maybe once an hour, sometimes less. Most families have one car per adult. Yes, really.

2. Neighbors share vegetables

Someone grows too many cucumbers? You'll find a bag at your door. Then you give something back. It's a never-ending cycle 😂

3. It's SO quiet at night

Except summer. Then the frogs in the rice fields are louder than Shibuya.

4. Garbage rules are serious

Burnable, plastic, cans, bottles, paper — each on a different day. Wrong day? Your bag stays there with a warning sticker. Everyone will know.

5. The convenience store is everything

Bank, post office, lunch, concert tickets, bills. In the countryside the konbini is the center of the town.

Slow, a little inconvenient… but I wouldn't trade it.

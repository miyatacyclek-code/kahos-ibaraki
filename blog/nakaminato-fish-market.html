Nakaminato Fish Market: Ibaraki’s seafood street

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

On TikTok I called it the fish market tourists don't know yet. Cheap, fresh, and so lively. It is my favorite seafood spot in Ibaraki: Nakaminato Fish Market (那珂湊おさかな市場) in Hitachinaka city.

It is not one big building. It is a row of fish shops and small restaurants next to the fishing port. Shop staff call out to you, the ice is full of fish, and you can smell grilled seafood everywhere.

What to eat

Most people come for kaisendon, a bowl of rice with raw seafood on top. You can also find sushi, grilled fish sets and fried seafood. Many shops sell fish to take home, too.

Now is a good time to go. From late autumn to winter, Ibaraki is famous for ankō (monkfish). It is a big winter food here.

How to get there from Tokyo

Take the JR Joban Line limited express from Tokyo or Ueno to Katsuta (about 1 hour 15 minutes). Change to the small Hitachinaka Seaside Railway (Minato Line) and ride about 15 minutes to Nakaminato Station. Then walk about 10 to 15 minutes to the market. In total it is about 2 hours.

By car, it is about 5 km from Hitachinaka IC. There is a large prefectural parking lot next to the market, and it is very cheap.

Tips

Opening hours and closed days are different for each shop. Many open in the morning and close around 4 pm, so go early. Weekends and lunchtime are busy. Weekdays are calmer.

Hitachi Seaside Park is close by, so you can plan a sea day: fish market lunch, then the park in the afternoon.

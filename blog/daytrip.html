Easy one-day trip to Ibaraki from Tokyo

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Many travelers ask me: “Can I visit Ibaraki without a car?” Yes! Here's an easy route.

Morning: Hitachi Seaside Park

Take the JR Joban Line limited express from Tokyo/Ueno toward Katsuta, then the bus to the park. Go early — especially in spring!

Lunch: Nakaminato fish market

Fresh sushi and seafood bowls. It's a short ride from the park area. Come hungry.

Afternoon: Oarai Isosaki Shrine

See the torii gate on the rocks by the sea. Sunset here is beautiful too.

Tips

  • Check bus times before you go — they are not frequent
  • Bring cash for small shops
  • Buy hoshiimo as a souvenir (omiyage)

Then you can say: I've been to the real Japan!

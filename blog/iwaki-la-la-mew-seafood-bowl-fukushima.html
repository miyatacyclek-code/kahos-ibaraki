Iwaki La La Mew: a $19 seafood bowl by the sea in Fukushima

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

This week I posted a video with a big title: “Skip Tokyo. The best seafood in Japan is in the countryside.” Many people asked where it was, so here is the full story.

What is Iwaki La La Mew?

Iwaki La La Mew (いわき・ら・ら・ミュウ) is a big seafood building at Onahama Port in Iwaki, Fukushima. It is right by the sea, and it is just over the border from north Ibaraki. It was badly damaged in the 2011 earthquake and tsunami, but it came back. In September 2025, it reopened as an official michi-no-eki (roadside station).

The seafood bowl

I ordered a huge kaisendon (seafood bowl) with miso soup. It was about $19, and it was full of fresh fish. There was also a sea urchin and salmon roe bowl for about $42. I didn’t order it… but I looked at it for a long time 👀 Would you pay $42?

Fresh fish, a sea view, and a bowl bigger than my face.

Walk the fish market

After lunch, I walked around the seafood market on the first floor. There are many shops with fresh fish, crabs and dried fish, and the shop staff call out to you. You can also buy seafood and grill it yourself at the BBQ area. There is a souvenir street too, with local snacks from Iwaki.

On the second floor there is a small exhibition about the earthquake and how the town recovered. I think it is worth a few minutes.

Good to know

  • Opening hours: usually 9am to 6pm. Check the official site before you go.
  • Everything is indoors, so it is a good plan for a rainy day.
  • Aquamarine Fukushima, a big aquarium, is right next to it.
  • Weekends are busy at lunch time. Go early!

How to get there

  • By train and bus: take the JR Joban Line to Iwaki Station, then a bus to Onahama (about 40–50 minutes).
  • By car: take the Joban Expressway. From north Ibaraki it is an easy drive, so you can add it to a trip to Kitaibaraki or Takahagi.

Save this for your Japan trip, and tell me: would you visit rural Japan just for the food? I think you should 🍣

Karaage (Japanese Fried Chicken) 唐揚げ

by Kaho · Ibaraki, Japan

Potato starch is my secret. Crispy outside, juicy inside. Eat it just out of the oil!

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • 300 g Chicken thigh, bite-size
  • 1½ tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Sake
  • 1 tsp Grated ginger
  • 1 tsp Grated garlic
  • 4 tbsp Potato starch (katakuriko)
  • 1 cup Oil for frying
  • 1 Lemon wedge (optional)

How to make it

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces.
  2. Put the chicken in a bowl with soy sauce, sake, ginger and garlic. Mix well and leave it for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Add the potato starch and mix with tongs until every piece is coated.
  4. Heat the oil to about 170°C (340°F). Fry the chicken for 3–4 minutes, then take it out and rest 4 minutes.
  5. Fry again at about 180°C (355°F) for 1 minute until golden and crispy. Serve hot with lemon!

Living outside Japan?

No potato starch? Use cornstarch. No sake? Use water.
